# AgentOps AI: Pricing and Subscription Plans

## Overview
AgentOps AI offers flexible pricing tiers tailored for individual researchers, growing teams, and large enterprises. All plans include access to autonomous agent workflows and local RAG retrieval.

## Subscription Tiers

### 1. Starter Plan
- **Price**: $49 per month (or $490 billed annually)
- **Target Audience**: Individual analysts and solo researchers
- **Inclusions**:
  - Up to 1,000 agent queries per month
  - 5 custom document collections
  - Standard response latency
  - Community forum support

### 2. Professional Plan
- **Price**: $199 per month (or $1,990 billed annually)
- **Target Audience**: Business intelligence teams (up to 10 team members)
- **Inclusions**:
  - Up to 10,000 agent queries per month
  - Unlimited document ingestion and collections
  - Priority queue processing
  - Custom tool integration support
  - Email and chat support with 24-hour SLA

### 3. Enterprise Plan
- **Price**: Custom contract pricing based on query volume and infrastructure
- **Target Audience**: Global enterprises and high-compliance organizations
- **Inclusions**:
  - Unlimited agent queries and dedicated throughput
  - Dedicated VPC or on-premises deployment options
  - Custom embedding models and private vector stores
  - 99.9% uptime SLA and 24/7 dedicated account manager
  - SSO, SAML, and custom role-based access control (RBAC)
