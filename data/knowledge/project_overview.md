# AgentOps AI: Project Overview

## Mission Statement
AgentOps AI is an autonomous, production-oriented business intelligence and strategic research agent. Its mission is to transform complex user objectives into actionable, grounded, and auditable business insights. By coordinating multi-step planning, tool invocation, and knowledge retrieval, AgentOps AI minimizes hallucination and provides verifiable analytical outputs.

## Core Capabilities
AgentOps AI provides four foundational pillars of autonomous capability:
1. **Autonomous Tool Selection**: The agent decides dynamically when to call specialized computational or retrieval tools rather than relying on LLM guesswork.
2. **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Integrates an in-memory or persistent vector database (ChromaDB) to ground answers in verified company documentation.
3. **Deterministic Arithmetic**: Leverages a validated calculator tool to perform calculations safely without dangerous code execution.
4. **Structured Report Synthesis**: Produces structured executive briefings complete with source citations and data references.

## Target Audience and Use Cases
AgentOps AI is purpose-built for:
- **Corporate Strategy Teams**: Rapidly evaluating market opportunities, competitor landscapes, and unit economics.
- **Business Intelligence Analysts**: Automating data synthesis from internal documentation and knowledge repositories.
- **Executive Leadership**: Generating concise, grounded decision-support briefs on demand.
